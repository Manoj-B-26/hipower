Our Story

Engineering Excellence Since 2004

HIPOWER is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company with twenty years of rich experience in Power Management, Cooling Management, and IT Infra Management to a wide range of customers with a Pan India Service network.

We offer products & service support for the requirements of wide-ranging applications — from Manufacturing, Healthcare, Energy, Information Technology, Banking, Medical, Diagnostics, Pharma, and Engineering to Space Research and Atomic Power plants.

As a trusted partner of Vertiv (formerly Emerson Network Power), we bring world-class power and cooling solutions to organizations that demand zero downtime and absolute reliability.
