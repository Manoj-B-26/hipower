Part Number: 5.05101E+11
Manufacturer: VertivType: Double Conversion Online UPSUNSPSC: 39121011
The Vertiv Liebert Powerbank 600 RT UPS is a fully-digital, highly reliable, double-conversion UPS solution that provides clean and consistent power. This highly efficient solution is ideal for various deployments, including IT racks, network closets, automation control systems, and precision instruments to small sized control rooms among other edge applications. Its cutting-edge design enables seamless integration into various ecosystems. This 6kVA UPS offers single-phase input power for critical loads. It is tailored for global deployment in a low carbon, compact footprint. This efficient UPS offers up to >90% efficiency over a wide range of load conditions that contribute to OPEX cost savings.
Vertiv Liebert Powerbank 600 RT UPS 6kVA (1x1) - Fully digital | True Online double conversion | Compact Efficient Robust UPS for Critical Applications | Built-in Ethernet port | Compatibility with intelligent cards | SIC card RDU_SIC | Integrable SCADA and BMS | LI-ion battery compatible | Rack and tower convertible

Key Features

  • Protects UPS and load from high voltage fluctuations with inbuilt isolation transformer
  • Prevents passing on noise, harmonics, and distortion to connected loads or from being fed back
  • Transient voltage surge suppression provides additional protection for the connected equipment
  • Prolongs battery life by allowing the UPS to maximize use of utility power before transferring to battery
  • Built-in Ethernet port includes compatibility with intelligent cards with browser support
