Vertiv Liebert PDX PX051H Direct Expansion Cooling, Downflow Frontal Direction Cooling, Fixed, Precision Air Conditioning

Part Number: 5060T1051004
Manufacturer: VertivType: Cooling SystemUNSPSC: 40101700
The Vertiv Liebert PX051H is a direct expansion cooling unit designed for small and medium data centers. This 51kW precision cooling unit has Liebert EC fans 2.0 that helps prolong the lifespan of your critical equipment by protecting them from overheating. The PDX units help optimize server room temperature, which helps reduce the risk of equipment damage from high temperature and humidity. This reduces the need for costly repairs and replacements, helping you save money over time. Each PDX comes with a Vertiv iCOM device designed to manage the operation of PDX units. Connect multiple PDX units in the same room with the facility's ethernet and access multiple units from one screen. To save you time and effort, the iCOM's Teamwork Mode can automatically supervise and adjust the operation of multiple units. The different Freecooling modes helps the PDX adapt to various cooling demands. This helps the PDX suit your scaling and evolving business needs.

Key Features

  • Vertiv Liebert PX051H: 51kW, downflow frontal, fixed cooling capacity, (wxdxh) 1750x890x1970mm
  • Comes with digital scroll, R410A refrigerant, Electronic Expansion Valve (EEV), and EC Fans 2.0
  • The iCOM controller allows PDX units to operate in Teamwork Mode for energy-efficient load sharing
  • Different Freecooling modes help enhance adaptability to various site requirements
  • Comes in air-cooled and water-cooled versions, helps manage a wide range of temperatures
