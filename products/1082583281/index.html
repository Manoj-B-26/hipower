Vertiv Avocent SVKM140 Desktop KM Switch | 4 Ports | USB Keyboard Mouse |USB 3.0

Part Number: SVKM140
Manufacturer: VertivType: KM SwitchboxUNSPSC: 43211601
The Vertiv Avocent SVKM140 is a 4-port Desktop KM switch that helps your organization achieve greater productivity at lower cost. With cursor navigation switching (CNS), zero-delay switching, managed copy and paste, multiple monitor support and presets, and a high-speed mobile device charging port, the SVKM140 KM switch streamlines connectivity as a front end to one or more high performance solutions. All this functionality inside a console no bigger than a keyboard and can be easily mounted underneath the desktop.
Vertiv Avocent SVKM140 Desktop KM Switch | 4 Ports | USB Keyboard Mouse |USB 3.0 - Vertiv SVKM 100 | Desktop KM | Switching Solutions | High-speed charging port | Multiple Monitor Support and Presets | Managed Copy-Paste | Cursor Navigation Switching

Key Features

  • 4-port module with 3 front panel USB ports grants simultaneous access to all required information
  • High-speed charging port for charging mobile phones or other devices
  • Emulation of instant mouse and keyboard switching assures zero-delay switching between systems
  • Switch control between systems by moving the mouse cursor from one display monitor to another
  • Copy and paste content from one system to another by moving the cursor to another system's monitor
