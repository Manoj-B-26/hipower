The Vertiv Edge uninterruptible power supply (UPS) is designed to protect server and networking equipment in distributed and Edge IT applications. With compact 1U rack format, this 500VA UPS offers reliable protection from downtime and damage caused by power spikes, swells and brownouts. Automatic Voltage Regulation (AVR) extends battery life and safeguards critical equipment. With sine wave output, this line interactive UPS ensures availability of network closets and Edge data centers. It operates at up to 98% efficiency in normal operation mode, providing energy savings. Add optional Vertiv Intellislot SNMP/Webcards for remote power management and OS's shutdown with free Vertiv Power Assist software. The Edge family has a wide range of power ratings, from 500VA up to 3000VA and form factors, such as mini-tower, 1U rack and rack/tower. It is easy to install, configure and operate and comes with an intuitive, color LCD screen. It has a standard 3-year Vertiv UPS warranty.
