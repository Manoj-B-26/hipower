Vertiv Liebert GXT MT+ EX UPS | 3kVA | True Online | Long-Run| Double Conversion

Part Number: 5.266E+11
Manufacturer: VertivType: Double Conversion Online UPSUNSPSC: 39121011
The Vertiv Liebert GXT MT+ EX uninterruptible power supply (UPS), with a capacity of 3kVA, is a compact and efficient UPS solution to help ensure the health and availability of IT infrastructure. With online, double conversion technology, the long run Vertiv Liebert GXT MT+ EX UPS protects critical equipment against outages and every type of power disturbance without drawing on battery power, extending battery life and minimizing replacements. This UPS satisfies safety criteria and electromagnetic compatibility standards while offering intelligent monitoring and network management functions. It comes in standard and long backup models. This high-performance UPS is ideal for data networks / small-sized data centers/VOIP applications and process automation equipment. With proven reliability, it is suited for critical applications in banking, financial and insurance, education, government, healthcare, retail and wholesale and telecom industries.
