Vertiv Liebert PDX PX041U Direct Expansion Cooling, Up Flow Direction Cooling, Digital, Precision Air Conditioning

Part Number: 5060T1041002
Manufacturer: VertivType: Cooling SystemUNSPSC: 40101700
The Vertiv Liebert PX041U is a direct expansion cooling unit designed for small and medium data centers. This 41kW precision cooling unit has Liebert EC fans 2.0 that helps prolong the lifespan of your critical equipment by protecting them from overheating. The PDX's Digital Scroll helps you precisely control and optimize your server room temperature and humidity. This helps your IT equipment run faster while lowering the risk of overheating. This reduces the need for costly repairs and replacements, helping you save money. Each PDX comes with a Vertiv iCOM designed to manage the operation of PDX units. Connect multiple PDX units in the same room with the facility's ethernet and access multiple units from one screen. To save you time and effort, the iCOM's Teamwork Mode can automatically supervise and adjust the operation of multiple units. The different Freecooling modes helps the PDX adapt to various cooling demands. This helps the PDX suit your scaling and evolving business needs.
